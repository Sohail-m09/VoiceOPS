
# Warehouse SOP Documents

Place warehouse Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) documents in this folder.

## Supported formats

- PDF (.pdf)
- Microsoft Word (.docx)
- Plain text (.txt)

## Example documents

- Inventory Management SOP
- Order Processing SOP
- Shipment and Dispatch SOP
- Warehouse Safety Guidelines
- Returns and Damaged Goods Policy

## Important

Use synthetic or publicly shareable documents for this portfolio project.

Do not commit confidential company documents, customer information, credentials, or internal business policies.
